NIVO

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

NIVO is built so that we don’t need your personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don’t run servers that store information about you, and we never sell or share data for advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, NIVO keeps the following on your device: the first name you choose to enter (optional), your wake-up time and morning window, your workout results, skill levels, streak, medals and app settings.

iCloud backup

If iCloud is turned on, NIVO saves a copy of that same progress in your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it comes back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we can’t see or access it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, card or payment details. To confirm that a subscription is active, NIVO uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your App Store purchase receipts (products, prices, dates, renewal status), and basic technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, country/region and IP address. This is used only to validate and restore purchases. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Morning reminders, streak reminders and the trial reminder are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server and you can turn them off at any time in Profile or in iOS Settings.

Guard (Screen Time)

If you turn on Guard, you choose the apps to pause through Apple’s Screen Time controls. Apple keeps those selections private: NIVO only receives encrypted tokens, never the names of the apps, and the selection never leaves your iPhone. To show you how many app opens Guard paused, NIVO counts those events on your device only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete My Data to erase everything from your iPhone and from your iCloud backup. If you use NIVO on more than one device, do this on each one. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone; the iCloud copy stays in your iCloud account until you use Delete My Data. Your subscription is managed by Apple and isn’t affected.

Children

NIVO is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
