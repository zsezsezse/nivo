How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. In NIVO open Profile → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NIVO. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You won’t be charged during the trial, and if notifications are on, NIVO reminds you two days before it ends. If you don’t cancel, the yearly price is charged when the trial ends.

I paid but NIVO Pro isn’t active

Open Profile → Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe. If that doesn’t help, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app or change phones?

No. If iCloud is on, your progress is backed up to your own iCloud account and comes back when you install NIVO again with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Delete My Data. This erases your history, streak, medals and settings from the iPhone and from your iCloud backup.
